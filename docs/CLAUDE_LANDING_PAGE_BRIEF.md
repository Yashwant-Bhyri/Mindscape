# MindScape Landing Page Brief

Use this when the task is specifically to redesign the landing page, not the whole app.

## Goal

Create a landing page that can be shown publicly and feels worthy of a serious, premium mental-health product.

The current landing page is too much of a role gateway and too aware of the underlying migration story. The redesign should turn it into a category-defining product reveal.

## Product truth

MindScape is a mental-health clinical operating system that connects:

- live session intelligence
- between-session patient support through Nancy
- clinician review and handoffs
- escalation and SOS pathways
- clinician research and community through Doctor's Corner

This is not a generic AI chatbot product.

## Active stack clarification

- The landing page being redesigned is the `Next.js` landing page in `frontend/`.
- `FastAPI` is the active backend contract.
- Mesop is legacy repo context only and should not influence the final public-facing landing-page copy.

## Core narrative

The landing page should tell this story:

1. Mental healthcare still breaks down between visits.
2. Diagnosis is too often delayed, fragmented, or context-poor.
3. Between-session deterioration gets buried in notes, messages, or missed entirely.
4. Patients need more humane support.
5. Clinicians need a better operating system, not more noise.
6. MindScape connects sessions, support, handoffs, risk review, and clinical intelligence into one system.

## Tone

Aim for:

- premium
- editorial
- clinically credible
- emotionally intelligent
- elegant
- calm but ambitious

Avoid:

- startup-demo energy
- admin-tool language
- engineering-stack copy
- flashy motion overload
- generic wellness cliches

## User-requested direction

The landing page should:

- feel legendary, like a serious product reveal
- include restrained floating problem statements or headline motion around current mental-health failures
- introduce MindScape's mission in a layered way
- feel like revealing the system piece by piece
- spotlight Nancy AI
- spotlight Doctor's Corner
- explain the technical sophistication in product language
- make the chat/care surfaces feel compassionate and immaculate

## Recommended page structure

### 1. Hero

Purpose:

- establish category, mission, and emotional seriousness

Needs:

- strong headline
- strong subhead
- premium art direction
- primary CTA
- secondary CTA if needed

Do not lead with:

- role gateway copy
- stack details
- migration language

### 2. The problem

Purpose:

- frame the broken mental-health status quo

Can include:

- restrained moving quotes or subtle floating lines
- short, sharp statements around delay, misdiagnosis, poor reach, irrelevant treatment, and fragmented continuity

### 3. What MindScape changes

Purpose:

- introduce the product thesis

Suggested themes:

- intelligence that stays grounded in the patient journey
- continuity between visits
- a system that supports both doctor and patient

### 4. Layered reveal of the product

Recommended sequence:

1. live session intelligence
2. Nancy between visits
3. clinician review and alerts
4. escalation path
5. Doctor's Corner and intelligence network

The reveal should feel like "unfolding MindScape", not a flat feature grid.

### 5. Signature features

Most important:

- Nancy AI
- patient-aware session analysis
- Doctor's Corner

### 6. Technical credibility

This should exist, but in product language.

Explain:

- multimodal session analysis
- longitudinal context
- structured handoffs
- evidence and signal synthesis

Avoid:

- raw framework names as primary value props
- developer changelog energy

### 7. Role-specific continuation

Only after the story is established should the page help users continue into:

- clinician path
- patient path

The role split should feel like a graceful continuation, not the whole page's identity.

## Copy directions

### Should sound like

- "MindScape brings intelligence, continuity, and compassion into mental-health care."
- "Between visits is where too much mental-health care breaks down."
- "A care operating system for clinicians, patients, and the signals that usually get lost."

### Should not sound like

- "Choose the right entrance"
- "What changed"
- "No longer depends on Mesop"
- "Frontend: Next.js 16"
- "Backend: FastAPI"
- "real routeable web shell"

## Visual directions

### Good directions

- elegant motion
- strong typography
- editorial layout
- soft but precise color and atmosphere
- premium product storytelling
- subtle transition from systemic problem to care confidence

### Bad directions

- noisy animated dashboard backgrounds
- futuristic sci-fi overload
- too much glass everywhere
- generic AI gradients
- cheerful wellness branding that ignores clinical seriousness

## Must preserve in the story

- MindScape is clinician-supervised, not clinician-replacing
- Nancy is a care companion, not a therapist or emergency responder
- the product is ambitious but should not be overclaimed as fully mature hospital infrastructure

## Suggested output from Claude Design

Produce a landing-page redesign that:

- can be pitched publicly
- tells the full MindScape story
- feels world-class
- uses sharper, more meaningful placeholder copy everywhere
- sets up the rest of the product as premium, serious, and humane
